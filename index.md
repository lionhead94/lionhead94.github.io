## About Me

I am a Ph.D. student at [DIBRIS][dibris], [University of Genoa][unige].
I am a member of the [Perception and Interaction Lab][pilab], and I work under the guidance of Prof. [Manuela Chessa][manuela] and Prof. [Danilo Pani][danilo] (University of Cagliari).

## Research Interests
- **Extended Reality**
- **Human-Computer Interaction**
- **VR-Based Rehabilitation**
- **Computer Vision**
